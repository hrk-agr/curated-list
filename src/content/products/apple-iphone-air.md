The iPhone Air is Apple's boldest design statement in years — a 5.5mm thin smartphone that slips into any pocket yet packs the A18 chip, a stunning 6.6-inch Super Retina XDR display, and all-day battery life.

## Why We Recommend It

If you want the largest iPhone experience in the most pocketable form, the Air is unmatched. It sheds the bulk without sacrificing performance, making it ideal for people who carry their phone all day and want something that simply disappears.

## Key Features

- 5.5mm profile — the thinnest iPhone ever made
- 6.6-inch Super Retina XDR display
- A18 chip with Apple Intelligence support
- 48MP main camera with next-generation computational photography
- USB-C with faster transfer speeds
- Available in five finishes

## Who It's For

Minimalists, frequent travellers, and anyone upgrading from an older iPhone who wants a premium, ultra-thin device that still delivers flagship performance.

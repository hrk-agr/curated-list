The Keychron K2 strikes the perfect balance between compact size and usability. With its 75% layout, you keep the function row and arrow keys while saving significant desk space.

## Why We Recommend It

Mac users have historically had limited mechanical keyboard options. Keychron changed that with a dedicated Mac layout — Command and Option keys in the right positions, plus media controls that work out of the box with no remapping required.

## Key Features

- 75% layout with function row and arrow keys
- Wireless Bluetooth 5.1 (up to 3 devices) or USB-C wired mode
- Hot-swappable switches (on select variants)
- Available with Gateron G Pro Red, Brown, or Blue switches
- White LED or RGB backlighting options
- Up to 72-hour battery life
- Aluminum frame option for added rigidity

## Who It's For

Developers, writers, and Mac power users who want a compact, reliable mechanical keyboard without sacrificing the functionality of a full-size board.

The Herman Miller Aeron Chair has been the benchmark for ergonomic office seating since its introduction in 1994. Redesigned in 2016, it continues to set the standard for comfort, adjustability, and durability.

## Why We Recommend It

The Aeron's 8Z Pellicle suspension material contours to your body, distributing weight evenly and eliminating pressure points during long work sessions. The PostureFit SL back support stabilizes and gently tilts the sacrum and lumbar for a healthier seated posture.

## Key Features

- Three sizes (A, B, C) to fit a wide range of body types
- 8Z Pellicle mesh for breathability and pressure distribution
- PostureFit SL lumbar support
- Fully adjustable arms (4D)
- Forward tilt for active sitting
- 12-year warranty

## Who It's For

Remote workers, studio designers, engineers, and anyone who spends 6+ hours a day at a desk. The Aeron is a long-term investment in your health and productivity.
